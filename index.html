About

Uwem Mbosowo, Senior Associate, AideOne Consult Ltd, Lagos

Uwem Mbosowo is an accounting, tax and internal control professional in Lagos with over three years of practice across bookkeeping, financial reporting, donor fund accounting, tax compliance and audit support.

As a Senior Associate at AideOne Consult Ltd, he closes the books for several client companies each month, files their VAT, WHT and PAYE, and represents them before federal and state tax authorities. He also supports donor-funded client work, covering fund accounting, grant compliance and disbursement tracking. On assurance work he leads audit planning, builds risk and control matrices for ICFR engagements, tests controls and coordinates management letter responses.

He also builds the tools that make the work easier: an internal control workpaper suite under COSO 2013, and a multi-state tax compliance tracker in Excel.

Uwem handles end-to-end finance work while the business owner focuses on other areas of the business. Owners hand Uwem the finance work and get back organised records, filings made on time and controls that hold up under review.

FAQ

Common questions

What does pricing look like?

Pricing depends on the stage, the volume of transactions and the scope. A written proposal follows the first conversation.

How is the right stage chosen?

Startup suits early-stage companies with simple operations. Growing SME suits companies of roughly 20 to 50 staff with regular suppliers and customers. Established Business suits companies of more than 50 staff and multi-entity groups. The first call settles the match.

Does Uwem replace an auditor?

No. Statutory audit opinions and work reserved for licensed practitioners go to a qualified firm. Uwem prepares records, tests controls and works with auditors.

Does Uwem recommend and set up software?

Yes. Recommendations rest on cost, fit and support, and Uwem connects the chosen tools to the books.

Which systems does Uwem work in?

QuickBooks, NAVISION and Excel. Existing company systems stay in place where possible.

How does Uwem protect company information?

Records stay confidential and reach no one without the company's consent.

What happens in the first month?

A baseline review of books, tax position and processes, then an agreed list of improvements.

How fast is the reply?

One working day.
